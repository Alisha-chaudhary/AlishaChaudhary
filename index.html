Featured work

ssh-enum

A statistical re-investigation of CVE-2016-6210, the OpenSSH username enumeration timing side-channel, on a modern default Ubuntu Server. Timing data was collected with manual probing, Hydra and Metasploit, then tested with Welch's t-test and Cohen's d.

No statistically significant timing difference under the tested default PAM configuration.
